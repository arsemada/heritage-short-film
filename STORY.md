# ከልጅነቴ የተወሰደ ትዝታ
## የሚጠብቅ ጎረቤት

### 1. About the Story

This is a personal childhood memory inspired by an experience involving my younger brother.

The story explores how children's curiosity, exposure to adult behaviour, and the responsibility of a caring community can influence a child's decisions and future.

It is based on a real family experience and is told retrospectively from the narrator's perspective.

### 2. The Original Story

When we were children, our mother used to sell traditional drinks from our home. People regularly visited to buy drinks, talk, and spend time around the house.

Among our customers was a man who regularly smoked cigarettes while drinking. My younger brother often observed him and became curious about his behaviour without understanding the harm associated with smoking.

One evening, around sunset, my younger brother noticed a small discarded cigarette lying on the ground.

After checking that nobody was watching, he hurried towards the wall near our home. He placed one foot against the wall, attempting to imitate the way he had seen the customer standing while smoking.

However, as soon as he tried it, he started coughing.

At that moment, an older couple who were neighbours our family knew happened to be passing nearby. They noticed what was happening and immediately stopped him.

Instead of ignoring the situation, they took him home and informed our mother.

Our mother was deeply saddened. She explained to him how harmful cigarettes were and why he should never try them again.

From that day forward, he never touched a cigarette again.

Looking back, this experience taught me something important: protecting children is not only the responsibility of parents. It is also the responsibility of the community around them.

### 3. Central Message

**ልጅን መጠበቅ የወላጅ ብቻ ሳይሆን የማህበረሰብ ኃላፊነት ነው።**

Protecting a child is not only a parent's responsibility; it is also the responsibility of the community.

### 4. Cultural Proverb

**«ሳይቃጠል በቅጠል።»**

The proverb reinforces the importance of early guidance and intervention before a small mistake develops into a serious problem.

### 6. Story Accuracy and Confirmed Details

The following details must remain consistent throughout production:

- The story is inspired by a real experience involving the narrator's younger brother.
- The mother sold traditional drinks from home.
- The smoking customer was a regular customer.
- The neighbours were people the family already knew.
- The neighbours were an older married couple.
- The incident happened around sunset.
- The brother never touched a cigarette again after the incident.

Details that must not be invented:

- The exact age of the brother.
- The specific type of traditional drink.
- Names or identifying details of the people involved.
- Dialogue that was never confirmed.
- Additional events that did not happen.

### 7. Intended Audience

The film is intended for:

- Ethiopian families and communities.
- Parents and caregivers.
- Young people.
- Anyone interested in community responsibility and child protection.
